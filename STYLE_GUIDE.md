# MindMarket Vibrant - Design System

## Core Philosophy
A playful, human-centric, and vibrant aesthetic that balances energy with trust.

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors
- **Mind Green** (#8ed462): Used for primary actions, success states, and hero backgrounds.
- **Dark Olive** (#2c2e2a): The primary text color in light mode and background in dark mode.
- **Soft Beige** (#f5f1e4): The main background color for light mode, creating a warm, organic feel.

### Accent Colors
- **Coral** (#ff705d): High-impact accent for highlights, warnings, or secondary CTAs.
- **Blue** (#2ba0ff): Used for information, links, and supportive elements.
- **Lavender** (#ebc1ff): Decorative accent.

## Typography
**Font Family**: Inter, sans-serif

### Scale
- **Display**: Bold, tight letter spacing (-0.03em).
- **Heading**: Clean, high contrast.
- **Body**: Legible, generous line height (1.6).

## Component Styling

### Buttons
- **Shape**: Fully rounded (Pill-shaped, `rounded-full`).
- **Primary**: Green background (`bg-primary`) with Dark text (`text-primary-foreground`).
- **Secondary**: Transparent or light background with border.

### Cards
- **Shape**: Large border radius (`rounded-[1.5rem]` or `rounded-3xl`).
- **Style**: 
  - Light Mode: White background with subtle shadow.
  - Dark Mode: Dark gray background (`bg-card`).

### Layout
- **Spacing**: Generous padding (`p-6` to `p-8`).
- **Structure**: Fluid grids, emphasis on white space.

## Motion
- **Transitions**: Smooth, bouncy (spring physics).
- **Hover**: Scale up (`hover:scale-105`) or brightness shift.
